# analysis/

## Student-visa labor-market outcomes — replication

A replication of [`co237/student_visa_analysis_nscg_2023`](https://github.com/co237/student_visa_analysis_nscg_2023)
(`Student visa analysis.R`), comparing former **student-visa** holders (first U.S. visa = student,
`FNVSATP==3`) with **natives** (`FNVSATP=="L"`) on the 2023 NSCG, restricted to full-time/full-year
workers.

### Files
- `student_visa_replication.R` — reads `data/pcg23Public/epcg23.csv.gz`, rebuilds the derived
  variables, computes every result table, writes the two self-employment charts to
  `output/figures/`, and saves all results to `output/student_visa_results.rds`.
- `build_report.R` — turns those results into `output/student_visa_replication.pdf`
  (via `knitr::kable` + `tinytex`; no pandoc required).

### How to run (from the repo root)
```r
Rscript analysis/student_visa_replication.R   # compute results + charts
Rscript analysis/build_report.R               # render the PDF
```

### Requirements
R (≥ 4.x) with: `readr`, `dplyr`, `tidyr`, `ggplot2`, `scales`, `matrixStats`, `readxl`,
`knitr`, and `tinytex` (with a working TeX install: `tinytex::install_tinytex()` if needed).

### Notes
- Medians are survey-weighted (`WTSURVY`, via `matrixStats::weightedMedian`).
- Country-of-birth codes (`BTHST_TOGA`) are labeled using the TOGA crosswalk shipped in `data/`.
- The self-employment rate is self-employed workers (`EMTP` 12/13) as a share of *all* workers in the
  group: self-employed ÷ (self-employed + not).
- Federal-agency support: `GOVSUP` (any federal support) plus the "mark all that apply" `FS*` flags
  (`FSDOD` Defense, `FSDOE` Energy, `FSDED` Education, `FSNIH`, `FSHHS` HHS ex-NIH, `FSNASA`, `FSNSF`,
  `FSOT` other). Rates are the weighted share of full-time/full-year workers whose principal-job work is
  supported by each agency; agencies overlap and need not sum to the "any federal support" figure.
- Basic / applied research: `WABRSH` and `WAAPRSH` — spent at least 10% of a typical work week on that
  activity — as a weighted share of full-time/full-year workers.
- The research and federal-support measures are each shown two ways: by **current** student-visa status
  (`VSATP == 2`, held a student/training visa on the reference date) and by **first-entry** student-visa
  status (`FNVSATP == 3`), each compared with all other full-time/full-year workers.
- `output/student_visa_results.rds` is git-ignored (regenerated by the first script).
